Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

RidePilot AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and web dashboard.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address and password when you create an account.

Trip Data: Trip details you log including dates, fares, tips, mileage, pickup and dropoff locations, and platform information (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, etc.).

Expense Data: Business expense information you enter including amounts, categories, and descriptions.

Receipt Images: Photos of receipts you choose to scan using the Receipt Scanner feature. These images are processed by Claude AI (Anthropic) and are not permanently stored.

Usage Data: App usage patterns to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the RidePilot AI service, including trip tracking, earnings analysis, tax estimation, and AI-powered strategy recommendations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 compliant database provider. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. AI Processing

RidePilot AI uses Claude AI (by Anthropic) to power the AI Strategist, Pre-Shift Briefing, Screenshot Import, and Receipt Scanner features. When you use these features, relevant data is sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing. We do not share your personally identifiable information with Anthropic beyond what is necessary to provide these features.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Supabase (database), Anthropic (AI), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Apple/Google (payment processing). Each has their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

6. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time by navigating to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account within the App. Upon deletion, all your personal data including trips, expenses, and earnings history will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. Note that deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription — please cancel your subscription in your App Store settings before or after deleting your account.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

8. Children's Privacy

RidePilot AI is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your data, contact us at support@ridepilotai.com
